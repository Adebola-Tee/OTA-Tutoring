IB Maths tutoring fees

How much does online IB Maths tutoring cost

By Taiwo Oloyede19 October 20264 min read

An IB Maths tutoring quote is difficult to compare if it does not identify the course, lesson length and support included. AA at HL and AI at SL are not descriptions a parent can safely leave out of the enquiry, even when the immediate concern is simply a low test result.

At OTA Learning Studio, we discuss those details before confirming a fee privately. A free parent consultation lets you ask about online Maths lessons, class packages and available times. We do not advertise an assumed price range that may not describe the service your child needs.

Get the course details together first

Ask the school to confirm AA or AI, SL or HL and the intended assessment session. Add the current teaching topic and any teacher comments. A tutor can then discuss whether the first priority is an earlier prerequisite, current classwork or applying knowledge to unfamiliar questions.

The IB mathematics overview is an official reference for the available courses. A provider's quote should relate to the correct programme rather than a generic advanced mathematics package.

If support with an internal assessment is being discussed, clarify its educational purpose. Tutoring can help understanding and suitable mathematical methods; it should not become purchased authorship of the student's assessed work.

Compare contact time and preparation

Ask who teaches and how long each appointment lasts. A fee per session and a fee per hour are not directly comparable until the duration is clear. Also establish whether the teaching is individual or shared.

Consider a learner who calculates a probability but cannot explain the event it describes. Useful preparation involves choosing a task that exposes that misunderstanding, teaching the relationship and checking a fresh attempt. Ask how that individual planning fits the service.

A large collection of resources is not the same as feedback on your child's reasoning. Find out what work is selected between sessions, whether it is reviewed and how the review influences the next lesson.

Separate regular support from a focused short plan

One student may need ongoing help alongside school. Another may have a limited difficulty with functions or interpreting a statistical model. The appropriate commitment should reflect that distinction.

Ask for a proposed learning priority and review point before agreeing to a long package. More appointments do not guarantee more useful learning if the same explanation is repeated without independent practice.

Our article on IB Maths HL lesson frequency explains what to discuss when the timetable is demanding. Include the student's other subjects and realistic practice time in the conversation, not only the approaching exam date.

Clarify the total before paying

Request the fee, session count and period covered by the proposed arrangement. Confirm payment expectations, rescheduling, missed lessons and any package conditions. A four-session plan is different from an undefined monthly commitment.

If you are enquiring from another time zone, give your location or zone and suitable local times. A slot that looks convenient in one country may be unsuitable for the learner's school day in another. Confirm the actual appointment time before booking.

Do not assume a package automatically offers a discount, unlimited marking or extra contact. Ask what is included and base the comparison on those terms.

Decide what would make the support worthwhile

A useful early goal might be interpreting an unfamiliar graph, justifying a calculus step or choosing a probability method without prompts. Ask how the tutor will gather evidence that this is improving.

Parents can look for clearer explanations and more independent attempts alongside school results. No initial quote can establish a guaranteed final IB grade, and a high fee alone cannot establish teaching quality.

Bring recent work to your consultation with OTA Learning Studio. We can discuss a possible starting focus, paid Google Meet lesson arrangements and fees, so that you understand the service before choosing a class package. A clear price belongs beside a clear teaching purpose.
