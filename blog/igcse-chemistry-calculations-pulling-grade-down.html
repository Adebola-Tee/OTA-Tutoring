IGCSE Chemistry Advice

IGCSE Chemistry Calculations Are Pulling My Child’s Grade Down — What Can Help?

By Taiwo Oloyede22 September 20262 min read

A student may understand reactions and practical work yet lose heavily on IGCSE Chemistry calculations. These questions combine chemical meaning, equations, ratios, units and algebra, so one weak stage can derail the entire solution.

Identify where the method fails

Check whether your child struggles to:

  • write or balance the equation
  • convert mass and amount
  • use mole ratios
  • calculate concentration or gas volume
  • handle percentage yield or purity
  • convert units and standard form
  • decide which information is relevant

Target the exact stage instead of assigning another random calculation worksheet.

Begin with the chemical story

Before using a formula, ask: What substance is given? What must be found? How are the substances connected by the reaction?

Writing the balanced equation and marking the relevant quantities makes the route visible.

Use a consistent structure

  1. Write the chemical equation or relationship.
  2. Convert the given value into the required quantity.
  3. Apply the chemical ratio.
  4. Convert to the requested answer.
  5. Add units and sensible precision.

Clear working helps students diagnose mistakes and may preserve method marks.

Build one skill at a time

Practise several questions on one calculation type before mixing methods. Once accuracy is secure, introduce unfamiliar wording and timed work.

Cambridge students can use the official Chemistry 0620 past papers and examiner reports for board-aligned practice.

If mole ratios are the main obstacle, continue with why IGCSE students get stuck on moles and stoichiometry.
