Does My Child Need an IGCSE Physics Tutor? 7 Signs to Look For

By Taiwo Oloyede22 September 20262 min read

One difficult topic does not automatically mean your child needs an IGCSE Physics tutor. However, repeated problems can become harder to repair when they are left until the final months.

These seven signs suggest that focused support may help.

1. The lesson makes sense, but the questions do not

Your child follows classroom examples yet freezes when the diagram or wording changes.

2. Formula knowledge is not producing marks

They remember equations but choose the wrong one, mishandle units or cannot explain the answer physically.

3. The same mistakes keep returning

Corrections are understood on the day, but similar questions go wrong in the next test.

4. Earlier gaps are blocking new topics

Weak graph skills, algebra, forces or energy knowledge can affect several later chapters.

5. Past-paper scores have stopped improving

The student completes papers but records only totals, without diagnosing and repairing repeated errors.

6. Revision is mainly passive

Most study time is spent reading, highlighting or watching solutions rather than answering unseen questions.

7. Confidence is falling

Your child avoids Physics, leaves questions blank or assumes that improvement is impossible.

What should tutoring provide?

Useful tutoring should start with a marked paper, identify the precise mixture of concept, calculation and exam-technique weaknesses, and measure progress through independent work.

It must also match the student’s exam board. Cambridge families can check the official Physics 0625 course information, while Edexcel students should follow the Pearson specification used by their centre.
