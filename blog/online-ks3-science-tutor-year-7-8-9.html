KS3 Science Tutoring

Online KS3 Science Tutor for Years 7–9: Biology, Chemistry and Physics

By Taiwo Oloyede26 September 20262 min read

If your child is in Year 7, 8 or 9, an online KS3 Science tutor should do more than prepare them for a distant GCSE. These years are where learners start connecting Biology, Chemistry and Physics ideas and learn how to explain evidence.

England's KS3 science curriculum develops scientific understanding across all three disciplines. The exact sequence varies by school; ask the student which topics they have covered rather than starting with a generic GCSE paper.

Identify the source of the difficulty

An incorrect answer may reflect a missing concept, unfamiliar vocabulary, an inability to read a graph or a weak explanation. For instance, a student may remember that particles move yet struggle to explain why heating changes the rate of diffusion. Ask them to draw or describe what happens at particle level before asking for a polished written answer.

What should lessons include?

  • One short question to see what the student already thinks.
  • A clear explanation or model, followed by a chance to challenge it.
  • Practice with a diagram, table or unfamiliar example.
  • Feedback on the student's reasoning, not just the final answer.
  • A fresh question at the end to test whether the idea transfers.

Year 9 students who are beginning GCSE topics can also use this approach. It is easier to tackle equations or chemical reactions when the earlier concept is secure. Our article about why capable students can find GCSE Chemistry difficult gives one example.

Choosing a tutor

Ask whether the tutor can work across the specific disciplines your child needs, and share the school's scheme of work or a recent test. If one strand needs attention, say so: “Year 8 Physics, circuits and graph interpretation” is much more useful than “Science help.”
