Swiss Matura Advice

Swiss Matura Winter 2027: Registration Deadline, Exam Dates and Preparation Guide

By Taiwo Oloyede3 September 20263 min read

Students planning to sit the Swiss Matura Winter 2027 session now have an important date to work towards: 1 December 2026.

That is the official registration deadline for the Winter 2027 Swiss Matura examination session. With the main written examinations beginning in February 2027, students still have time to prepare — but September is the point to turn preparation into a structured plan.

Swiss Matura Winter 2027 registration deadline

The official Swiss Matura Winter 2027 registration deadline is 1 December 2026.

Registration is completed online. Candidates should avoid leaving the process until the final days, particularly if documents still need to be prepared or submitted.

According to the official schedule, the complete registration dossier must also be sent to the State Secretariat for Education, Research and Innovation (SERI/SBFI) by post no later than the first working day after the registration deadline.

Swiss Matura Winter 2027 exam dates

The Winter 2027 examination schedule depends on whether a candidate is completing the first part, second part or the full examination.

Key dates currently published include:

  • Second-part written examinations: 15–19 February 2027
  • First-part written examinations: 22–24 February 2027
  • Second-part oral examinations: 8–11 March 2027
  • First-part examinations: additional examinations take place within the published session period

The written examinations are expected to take place in Zurich, with oral examinations expected in Pfäffikon, Schwyz.

Candidates should remember that small adjustments to examination dates may still occur, so the official timetable sent to each student should always take priority.

How should students prepare for the Winter 2027 Swiss Matura?

With several months remaining, preparation should be systematic rather than rushed.

Start by identifying three things:

  • subjects where your foundations are weakest
  • topics that consistently cost you marks
  • subjects requiring significant written or problem-solving practice

From there, build weekly revision around the official syllabus instead of simply rereading notes.

Past examination questions are particularly useful because they help students become familiar with the level of reasoning, written explanation and time management expected in the Schweizerische Maturitätsprüfung.

Do not wait until January to start serious preparation

January may look far away, but students preparing across several Matura subjects can quickly run out of time.

A better approach is to use September to November to strengthen knowledge and complete syllabus gaps, then use the final months for past papers, timed practice and exam technique.

Students who are already struggling in Mathematics, Sciences or other demanding subjects should address those weaknesses early.

A simple Winter 2027 preparation timeline

September–October: identify weaknesses and strengthen core knowledge.

November: increase examination-question practice while completing registration requirements.

By 1 December: make sure your Swiss Matura registration is completed.

December–January: focus heavily on past papers, corrections and timed practice.

February–March: examination period.

The goal is not to study everything at once. It is to reach February knowing that the difficult topics have already been dealt with.

Planning to sit the Swiss Matura in 2027?

The 1 December 2026 Swiss Matura registration deadline may still be a few months away, but preparation for the Winter 2027 examination should already be underway.

Register on time, understand your examination schedule and use the remaining months strategically.
