Cambridge IGCSE vs Edexcel International GCSE Results 2026: What Parents in Asia Need to Know

By Taiwo Oloyede1 September 20265 min read

If your child attends an international school in Asia, you may have heard both names repeatedly: Cambridge IGCSE and Pearson Edexcel International GCSE.

Now that the 2026 IGCSE results are out, many parents are asking the same questions. Is one board harder? Why are the grades different? Can you compare an Edexcel grade 9 directly with a Cambridge A\*?

The short answer is that Cambridge and Edexcel are both respected international qualifications, but they are separate exam boards with different grading systems, specifications and assessment structures.

Cambridge IGCSE vs Edexcel Results Dates 2026

The two boards did not release their June 2026 results on the same day.

Cambridge IGCSE results were released on 18 August 2026, while Pearson Edexcel International GCSE results were released to students on 20 August 2026.

So if students in the same international-school community received results at different times, it may simply be because they sat different exam boards.

Cambridge IGCSE vs Edexcel: How Are Grades Awarded?

One of the biggest differences parents notice is the grading scale.

Pearson Edexcel International GCSE generally uses the 9–1 grading scale, with grade 9 representing the highest performance.

Cambridge IGCSE commonly uses the A\*–G scale, although some Cambridge syllabuses are also available with 9–1 grading.

This means parents should avoid comparing two students simply by looking at the symbols on their result slips.

An A\* under one system and a grade 9 under another both represent very strong achievement, but the grades come from different specifications and awarding processes.

Why Are Cambridge and Edexcel Grade Boundaries Different?

This is completely normal.

Cambridge publishes grade thresholds, while Pearson Edexcel publishes grade boundaries for each examination series.

The required mark for a particular grade can change depending on the subject, paper and difficulty of that examination series.

That means your child's 2026 result should only be compared with the official 2026 threshold or grade boundary for the exact subject and exam board they sat.

Do not compare:

  • Cambridge marks with Edexcel boundaries
  • One subject's boundary with another
  • 2026 marks with an old year's boundary without checking the current figures
  • Different syllabus or specification codes

Is Cambridge IGCSE Harder Than Edexcel?

There is no reliable universal answer.

Searches such as “Cambridge vs Edexcel which is harder?” are extremely common, but the answer depends on the subject and the individual student.

The boards may differ in:

  • question style
  • syllabus structure
  • coursework requirements
  • examination timing
  • tiering in certain subjects
  • how particular topics are assessed

Both qualifications are widely recognised internationally, so parents should focus less on which board sounds harder and more on whether their child performed well within the qualification they actually studied. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

What Should Parents Check After IGCSE Results?

If a grade is lower than expected, check these four things first.

The exact exam board Confirm whether your child sat Cambridge International or Pearson Edexcel.

The syllabus or specification code Two courses with similar names may still have different papers and grade boundaries.

The student's marks Check the overall and component marks where available.

The official 2026 grade boundary or threshold Find out how far the student actually was from the next grade.

This gives you a much clearer picture than simply looking at the final letter or number.

What If the Result Is Lower Than Expected?

A lower grade does not automatically mean the paper should be remarked.

If the student was only one or two marks away from the next grade, it may be worth speaking to the school about the script and available post-results services.

If the student was significantly below the next boundary, however, the priority may be identifying academic gaps and preparing more effectively.

Does Cambridge vs Edexcel Matter for Future Study?

In most cases, what matters more is the student's subjects, grades and the entry requirements of their next programme.

Both Cambridge IGCSE and Pearson Edexcel International GCSE are established qualifications used by international schools and recognised for progression to further study. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

So rather than worrying about which board looks more prestigious, parents should ask:

  • Did my child achieve the grades required for the next stage?
  • Are any key subjects below the required level?
  • Are there weaknesses that should be addressed before A Levels, IB or another programme begins?

What Parents in Asia Should Take Away

Cambridge IGCSE and Edexcel International GCSE are not the same exam board, so their results should not be compared as though they use identical papers or grading systems.

Check the correct 2026 grade threshold or boundary, understand how close your child was to the next grade, and then decide whether any action is actually necessary.
