SEAG Transfer Test

Online SEAG Maths and English tutoring before November

By Taiwo Oloyede20 September 20264 min read

Updated 14 October 2026.

For a P7 family considering SEAG tutoring in October, the decision is practical: what should a lesson improve before November? At OTA Learning Studio, we review attempted work, choose a small number of priorities and teach Maths or English skills that the child can use independently. The plan should fit the remaining time without filling every evening with papers.

The official SEAG dates place Paper 1 on 14 November and Paper 2 on 21 November 2026. Registration is closed. A free parent consultation can help you discuss paid online lessons, suitable times and the fee privately before booking. Tuition is separate from your child's examination application.

Decide what help is actually needed

Bring a recent attempted practice paper with the original choices and working. Note which questions were guessed, changed or left blank. A low total may hide a few repeated errors; a high total can still leave one specific weakness worth addressing. Tell us how the child feels about practice as well as the score.

We can discuss Maths support, English support or priorities across both. It is rarely helpful to promise every topic will be covered in the remaining weeks. Choose work that responds to the child's evidence and protects time for school, rest and ordinary family life.

Look at a Maths example before choosing more papers

Here is an original teaching question. A school trip costs £18 per child. A class collects £216. How many children have paid? Dividing 216 by 18 gives 12. Ask the child to explain why division fits the situation rather than simply naming the calculation.

Now suppose two more children join and each pays £18. The total increases by £36 to £252, representing 14 children. This follow-up checks whether the learner connects the number of groups with the total. A child who adds two pounds instead may need help with the meaning of the quantities, not harder division exercises.

The lesson should include a fresh problem with different wording. If the child can only solve the version just demonstrated, another supported example may be needed before independent practice or a time limit.

Use English evidence as carefully as numbers

Try this original passage: “Leah closed the gate gently. The puppy was asleep beside the kitchen door, and she did not want to wake him.” Why did Leah close the gate gently? The passage supports avoiding waking the puppy. It does not tell us that the gate was broken or that someone had forbidden noise.

We ask the child to point to the supporting words and reject a tempting unsupported answer. Then we use a new paragraph. For vocabulary, grammar or punctuation difficulties, the teaching priority changes. Our SEAG English tutoring guide explains those reading and language decisions in more detail.

Practise timing after checking the method

SEAG combines English or Gaeilge and Maths in each main assessment. Use the current official assessment guide and practice materials when planning familiarisation. A tutor should consider how time spent on one question affects the remainder of the paper, while still teaching the skill behind an error.

Begin with a manageable selection and review the decisions. Add timed sections when the learner has a reliable approach. A full paper every day can produce plenty of scores without resolving the same misunderstanding. Keep feedback specific, such as recognising the right quantity or supporting an inference with evidence.

Know how online lessons would work

Lessons take place through Google Meet. Agree how the child's written attempts will be shown, and keep paper and a pencil ready. A parent can help with the setup while allowing the learner time to answer without being coached through every step.

Between lessons, a small set of purposeful questions can be more useful than a large unfinished worksheet. Our free SEAG revision plan offers a structure you can adapt to the weeks remaining; the resource does not replace teaching or the official guidance.

Talk through a focused plan

Bring one paper, the two most persistent difficulties and realistic lesson times to your free consultation. We can discuss the fit for paid Maths or English support, how progress would be reviewed and the agreed fee. Read our parent feedback and ask how the proposed work responds to your own child's needs.
