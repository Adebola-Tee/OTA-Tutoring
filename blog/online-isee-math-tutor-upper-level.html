ISEE Preparation

Online ISEE Math Tutoring for Upper Level School Entry

By Taiwo Oloyede1 November 20264 min read

An online ISEE Math tutor should help a student explain unfamiliar problems, choose efficient methods and work independently. Strong school grades can be a useful starting point, but they do not show how comfortably a learner handles an admissions assessment under time pressure.

At OTA Learning Studio, we offer online Maths teaching that can be tailored to ISEE preparation. We will discuss the learner's needs before suggesting paid Google Meet lessons and agreeing fees privately.

Choose the level using the entry grade

The Upper Level ISEE is for applicants to Grades 9 to 12. The level follows the grade sought for admission, not simply the grade the student currently attends. Confirm requirements with each target school and the official ISEE information.

For example, a student currently in Grade 8 applying to Grade 9 prepares for Upper Level. A different applicant may need Middle or Lower Level. Check this before buying resources or organising a lesson plan around a familiar school year label.

Find out what happens when the question changes

Take an original example. Three notebooks cost $7.50. At the same price per notebook, eight cost $20 because one costs $2.50. Now ask how many notebooks can be bought with $17.50. The learner must reverse the direction and obtains seven.

A student who can complete the first task but not the second may be copying an operation rather than interpreting the relationship. Ask them to label the unit price and explain which quantity is unknown. That explanation gives the tutor a precise starting point.

The next question should look different enough to check transfer. A transport fare or a rate problem can test the same underlying idea without repeating the notebook wording. Successful teaching develops a decision the student can reuse.

Teach reasoning alongside reliable calculation

ISEE Maths preparation should include both Quantitative Reasoning and Mathematics Achievement. We discuss the difference in our guide to the two Maths sections. A learner may need support with interpretation in one and mathematical methods in the other.

During lessons, ask which information matters, what can be represented and how an answer can be checked. Estimation, a small sketch or a carefully chosen example can sometimes be more efficient than a long calculation. The tutor should explain when that approach is valid.

Use original examples and appropriate official practice

A useful lesson can include original problems for teaching and approved practice resources for assessment familiarity. We do not present our own questions as actual ISEE items or claim to know a future paper. Ask how any practice score is being interpreted before treating it as a forecast.

Avoid relying on a memorised method for a single question shape. For instance, knowing how to find 20% of a number is different from identifying the original value after a percentage change. The tutor should vary what is given and ask the student to justify the setup.

Make online teaching active

The student should write, explain and make decisions during a Google Meet lesson. A shared solution can show reasoning clearly, but watching the tutor complete everything hides gaps. Include an independent problem before the lesson ends and a small follow up task afterwards.

Parents can ask for three concrete points: what was difficult, what was taught and what the learner completed alone. This makes it easier to judge whether the sessions fit the student without turning every lesson into an argument about a target score.

Prepare around the booked date

A December sitting can leave several weeks for focused preparation in November, depending on the actual booking. ISEE dates vary by location and testing option. Our December preparation guide explains how to work backwards from the family's confirmed date.

Choose a manageable schedule with time between lessons to practise. Near the test, rehearse pacing and practical arrangements as well as content. No lesson package guarantees a particular score or admission decision, so progress checks should remain specific and honest.

Start with the information that matters

For the consultation, bring the entry grade, target school requirements, test format and a recent Maths attempt with working. Personal school or pupil details do not need to be posted publicly. We can discuss the learning priorities directly with the family.
