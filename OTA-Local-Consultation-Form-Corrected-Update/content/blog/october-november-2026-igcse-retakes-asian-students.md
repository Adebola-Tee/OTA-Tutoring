If your **IGCSE results in 2026 were lower than expected**, the October/November examination series may give you an opportunity to improve your grade without waiting until the following summer.

For students across Asia — including Hong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia, China, Japan, South Korea and other international-school markets — both **Cambridge International and Pearson Edexcel** offer autumn examination opportunities for many IGCSE subjects.

However, the deadlines are close, the available subjects vary, and Cambridge and Edexcel do not run their November series in exactly the same way.

Here is what Asian students and parents need to know about **October/November 2026 IGCSE retakes**.

## Can You Retake IGCSEs in October or November 2026?

Yes.

Many IGCSE students can retake subjects during the **October/November 2026 examination series**, depending on their exam board and syllabus.

Cambridge International offers a large November examination series, while Pearson Edexcel offers a November International GCSE series for a range of subjects.

Students commonly consider a retake after:

- narrowly missing a required grade
- receiving a result below their predicted grade
- needing a stronger result for sixth form or university
- underperforming because of poor exam technique
- struggling with timing in the original examination
- realising that there were significant gaps in their preparation

But before you begin revising, confirm that your **exact subject and syllabus are available in the November series**.

## Cambridge IGCSE October/November 2026 Retakes

Cambridge International runs its main autumn examination series across **October and November**.

In some administrative zones, certain papers and practical assessments can begin even earlier.

That is why students should not assume that every Cambridge IGCSE examination takes place in November simply because it is called the November series.

Cambridge publishes separate November timetables for different administrative zones.

Your exact examination dates therefore depend on:

- your country
- your Cambridge administrative zone
- your syllabus
- your component combination

Students in different Asian countries may not necessarily sit the same paper at the same local time.

Your school or examination centre should provide your final timetable.

## What Is the Cambridge IGCSE Retake Deadline for November 2026?

For Cambridge students who took the **same syllabus in the June 2026 series**, the key November 2026 retake entry deadline is:

**21 September 2026**

Cambridge specifically defines a retake entry as a candidate entering the same syllabus in November that they entered in the preceding June series.

This later deadline gives students time to receive their June results before deciding whether to retake.

However, your school or exam centre may set an **earlier internal deadline**, so do not wait until 21 September to make contact.

If you are considering a Cambridge IGCSE retake, speak to your exams officer as soon as possible.

## Pearson Edexcel International GCSE November 2026 Retakes

Pearson Edexcel also has a November 2026 International GCSE examination series.

The final timetable includes subjects such as:

- Mathematics
- Biology
- Chemistry
- Physics
- English Language
- English Literature
- Business
- Economics
- Geography
- History
- Accounting
- ICT
- selected languages

Not every International GCSE qualification is available in every series, so students should check their exact qualification code before making plans.

## What Is the Edexcel International GCSE Entry Deadline for November 2026?

For the **November 2026 Pearson Edexcel International GCSE series**, the standard International GCSE entry deadline is:

**12 September 2026**

This deadline is particularly important because it comes very soon after summer results.

Schools and examination centres may also impose earlier internal deadlines.

If you are considering an Edexcel International GCSE retake, contact your school or examination centre immediately rather than waiting until mid-September.

## When Are Edexcel International GCSE Exams in November 2026?

Pearson's final November 2026 International GCSE timetable begins in late October.

The first examinations are scheduled from **27 October 2026**, with papers continuing through November.

For example, Pearson Edexcel Mathematics A Paper 1 is scheduled for **4 November 2026**, while Paper 2 is scheduled for **6 November 2026**.

Other subjects are spread across different dates throughout the examination series.

Always check the timetable for your exact subject code because examination dates differ between qualifications.

## Which Asian Students Can Take IGCSE Retakes?

IGCSE retakes are commonly taken by students attending:

- international schools
- British curriculum schools
- private schools offering Cambridge or Edexcel qualifications
- independent examination centres
- homeschool programmes
- private candidate centres

Students across Asia may include candidates from Hong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia, China, Japan, South Korea, Thailand, Indonesia, Vietnam, the Philippines and other international education markets.

Availability ultimately depends on whether a school or approved examination centre near you accepts your entry.

## Can Private Candidates Retake IGCSEs in Asia?

In many locations, yes.

If your previous school will not enter you for the examination, you may be able to register through an authorised private candidate centre.

When contacting a centre, ask:

- Do you accept private IGCSE candidates?
- Do you offer Cambridge, Edexcel or both?
- Is my subject available in November 2026?
- What is your registration deadline?
- What are the total examination fees?
- Are practical or speaking components available?
- When will I receive my final timetable?

Do this early.

Private candidate availability can be limited, particularly for subjects requiring practical, speaking or coursework components.

## Should You Request a Remark Before Retaking?

Possibly.

If you missed your target grade by only a very small number of marks, investigate your post-results options before automatically registering for another examination.

Ask your school for:

- your component marks
- the official grade boundary
- how far you were from the next grade
- available review or remark services
- the relevant deadline

A student who missed the next grade by one mark faces a different decision from a student who needs a substantial improvement.

However, because retake entry deadlines can arrive quickly, do not stop preparing while you wait for a review decision.

## When Is an IGCSE Retake Worth It?

A retake can be worthwhile when improving the grade has a clear academic benefit.

For example, you may need a stronger IGCSE result for:

- A-level subject entry
- IB subject choices
- sixth-form admission
- university applications
- scholarship requirements
- future academic progression

A retake may also be sensible if you know your previous performance did not represent your true ability.

## Should You Retake If You Were Only One Grade Away?

Potentially.

But first find out **why** you missed the grade.

If the problem was mainly careless mistakes or poor timing, focused exam practice could produce a meaningful improvement.

If you were weak across much of the syllabus, you may need more substantial preparation.

Do not choose a retake simply because you are unhappy with the letter or number on your results sheet.

Choose it because you have a realistic plan for improving the underlying performance.

## How to Prepare for an October/November IGCSE Retake

The time between August results and October/November examinations is relatively short.

That means your revision needs to be targeted from the beginning.

Start with a past paper or your previous examination performance.

Identify:

- topics where you consistently lose marks
- questions you struggle to interpret
- formulas or methods you forget
- weak practical or data-handling skills
- timing problems
- careless mistakes
- gaps in exam technique

Then build your revision around those weaknesses.

## Week 1: Analyse Your Previous Result

Do not immediately restart the syllabus from Chapter 1.

Look at your previous performance first.

If possible, obtain information about your component marks or scripts and identify where your grade was lost.

Create a list of your weakest topics.

## Weeks 2–3: Repair the Biggest Knowledge Gaps

Focus on the topics that are costing you the most marks.

Spend less time revising content you already understand well.

For Maths or Science subjects, this means completing questions rather than simply reading notes.

For essay-based subjects, practise planning and writing responses under realistic conditions.

## Weeks 4–5: Move Into Past-Paper Practice

Begin completing examination questions from your exact board and syllabus.

Use official mark schemes to identify:

- missing keywords
- incomplete working
- misunderstood command words
- weak explanations
- repeated calculation errors

Keep a record of your mistakes.

## Final Weeks: Practise Under Exam Conditions

As the retake approaches, complete full papers under timed conditions.

No notes.

No checking answers halfway through.

No stopping the timer.

You need to know how well you can perform when the examination conditions are realistic.

## Do Not Switch Randomly Between Cambridge and Edexcel Past Papers

This is especially important.

Cambridge IGCSE and Pearson Edexcel International GCSE are **not interchangeable examination specifications**.

Even when the subject content overlaps, question style, assessment structure and mark schemes can differ.

If you are retaking Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics, use the correct Cambridge syllabus and Cambridge papers.

If you are taking Edexcel International GCSE Mathematics A, practise the correct Pearson Edexcel Mathematics A papers.

Your revision should match the qualification you will actually sit.

## What Subjects Should You Prioritise for a Retake?

You do not necessarily need to retake every disappointing result.

Prioritise the subjects where a better grade matters most.

For example:

- a required Maths grade
- English needed for progression
- Sciences required for A-level study
- a subject connected to your intended university course
- a qualification required by your next school

Retaking several subjects at once can create a heavy workload.

A focused improvement in one or two important subjects may be more valuable than spreading your preparation too thinly.

## Cambridge or Edexcel: Which Retake Is Easier?

Neither exam board can universally be described as easier.

The better question is:

**Which qualification are you already prepared for?**

If you previously studied a Cambridge syllabus, switching suddenly to Edexcel simply because you believe it may be easier can create additional work.

The specifications, question styles and assessment requirements may be different.

Unless there is a strong academic reason to change qualification, continuing with the syllabus you already understand is often the more practical approach.

## When Will November 2026 IGCSE Retake Results Be Released?

Result dates depend on your exam board.

Pearson has scheduled **22 January 2027** as the results day for its November 2026 International GCSE series.

Cambridge publishes results information for each examination series separately, and students should confirm the applicable January 2027 results date through Cambridge or their examination centre.

Your school will normally explain when and how you can access your result.

## Important Advice for Asian Students Retaking IGCSEs

If you are planning an October/November 2026 IGCSE retake, do these things now:

- confirm your exam board
- identify your exact syllabus or qualification code
- check whether the subject is available in the November series
- contact your school or private examination centre
- confirm the entry deadline
- obtain your correct examination timetable
- begin targeted revision immediately

Do not rely on examination dates shared by students in another country.

For Cambridge candidates in particular, administrative zones matter.

The October/November IGCSE series can provide a valuable second opportunity for students who did not achieve the grade they wanted in the summer.

But the window between results day and the retake is short.

For Cambridge candidates who qualify as November retake entries, the key retake deadline is **21 September 2026**.

That means September is not the month to delay your decision.
