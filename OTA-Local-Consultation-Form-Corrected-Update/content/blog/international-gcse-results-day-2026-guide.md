International GCSE results are out, and for some students, Results Day brought exactly what they had hoped for.

For others, it brought questions.

**Was I close to the next grade? Should I ask for a review? Can I retake the subject? Will this result affect what I study next?**

If your 2026 IGCSE results were lower than expected, the best next step is not to panic. It is to understand the result properly before deciding what to do.

## When were International GCSE results released in 2026?

The date depends on your exam board.

**Cambridge IGCSE results** for the June 2026 series were released on **18 August 2026**. Cambridge reported that more than 315,000 students worldwide received Cambridge IGCSE and O Level results this year.

**Pearson Edexcel International GCSE results** were released on **20 August 2026**.

So before comparing your result with a friend's, make sure you are looking at information for the correct exam board and qualification.

## Your IGCSE result was lower than expected. What now?

Start with the actual grade.

Then ask:

- What grade did you need?
- How far were you from it?
- Does the grade affect your next course or school placement?
- Was the result very different from your usual performance?

A disappointing grade does not automatically mean you should retake the exam.

Sometimes the first sensible step is simply understanding your marks.

## Can you request a review of an IGCSE result?

Yes, post-results services are available.

Cambridge offers an **Enquiries About Results** process, which can include checking marking or moderation. Students normally need to speak to their school or exams officer first.

Pearson also offers post-results services for its Summer 2026 International GCSE series, including reviews of marking and access to scripts.

If you were only a small number of marks away from the grade you needed, speak to your school before deciding whether a review is appropriate.

A review is not a guaranteed grade increase.

## Should you remark or retake your IGCSE?

That depends on **why** the grade was lower than expected.

A review may be worth discussing if your performance was unusually low or you were extremely close to another grade.

A retake may make more sense if you already know there were gaps in your understanding or your exam performance was not strong enough.

Do not choose based only on disappointment.

Choose based on what the marks are telling you.

## If you decide to retake, change the preparation

A retake should not look exactly like the first attempt.

Identify what actually cost you marks.

For Maths, that might be algebra, problem solving, geometry or exam timing.

For English, it could be analysis, writing structure, comprehension or completing answers under time pressure.

Then make the preparation more targeted.

The aim is not to revise everything again.

It is to fix what prevented the stronger grade.

## Parents: focus on the next decision, not the disappointment

A student who has just received a disappointing result probably already knows it was not the grade everyone hoped for.

What they need next is clarity.

Find out what the school recommends, whether the grade affects their next step, and whether a review or retake would actually benefit them.

## International GCSE Results 2026 FAQs

### When were Cambridge IGCSE results released in 2026?

Cambridge IGCSE results for the June 2026 series were released on **18 August 2026**.

### When were Pearson International GCSE results released?

Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Summer 2026 results were released on **20 August 2026**.

### Can I ask for my IGCSE result to be checked?

Yes. Both Cambridge and Pearson provide post-results services, although the exact process and deadlines depend on the board and service requested.

### Should I retake an International GCSE?

Only if the grade matters for your next step and another attempt is likely to produce a better outcome. Speak to your school first and understand why you received the original grade.
