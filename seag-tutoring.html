Common questions

Before the first lesson.

Are lessons taught online?

Yes. Lessons are live and one-to-one, allowing your child to learn from home while receiving focused attention and immediate feedback.

Can you support both SEAG Maths and English?

Yes. Support covers both subjects and can concentrate more heavily on whichever area currently needs the most attention.

Can you help if my child is already using practice papers?

Yes. Practice-paper mistakes can be used to identify patterns, strengthen weak skills and improve timing and exam technique.

What happens during the free consultation?

You tell me about your child’s current preparation, difficulties and goals. I then recommend the most useful next step. There is no obligation to continue.
