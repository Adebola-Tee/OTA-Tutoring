Looking for an online Edexcel IAL Maths tutor in Hong Kong is easier when you know what the lessons need to achieve. A student preparing for Pure Mathematics 1 needs a different starting point from someone retaking Statistics 1 or working through Pure Mathematics 4. The course name alone does not tell a tutor enough.

The January 2027 series runs from 8 to 25 January through HKEAA. Confirm the date of each entered unit with your centre. ## Tell the tutor which units you are taking

Send the exact unit names and codes, your latest results and the grade you are working towards. Include whether each unit is a first attempt or a resit. If you are unsure of your entry, ask your school or examination centre rather than guessing from the textbook cover.

The [Pearson Mathematics specification](https://qualifications.pearson.com/en/qualifications/edexcel-international-advanced-levels/mathematics-2018.html) should guide the lesson content. International A Level Maths and the UK linear A Level are different qualifications. Resources should match your course and the units actually being assessed.

Bring one attempted question you found difficult. Working that stops halfway can reveal more than a list of chapters you dislike. It lets the tutor see where mathematical understanding, algebra or question interpretation needs attention.

## A first lesson should uncover the reason for an error

Consider this original differentiation example: y = 3x² − 12x + 5. The derivative is 6x − 12. Setting that equal to zero gives x = 2, and substitution into the original expression gives y = −7. The stationary point is therefore (2, −7).

A student might differentiate correctly but report only x = 2 when coordinates are requested. Another might substitute into the derivative instead of the original function. Those are different mistakes and need different feedback.

A useful tutor asks the learner to explain each step, checks the mathematical meaning and then changes the example. Merely watching a correct solution can create familiarity without building independent performance.

## Expect explanation followed by independent work

An online lesson should give the student time to write, make decisions and ask questions. Shared working is helpful, but the tutor should not carry every calculation. After discussing an example, the learner needs a fresh problem to attempt with less support.

For the differentiation task, try y = 2x² − 8x + 1. Ask the student to find the stationary point, explain which equation gives the x coordinate and check the y coordinate. This tests whether the earlier correction transfers.

Homework should revisit the same skill alongside older material. A short task completed properly is more informative than a large worksheet copied from a worked solution. Keep incorrect attempts so the next lesson can address the cause.
