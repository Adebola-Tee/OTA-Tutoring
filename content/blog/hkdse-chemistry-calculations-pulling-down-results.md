A student can understand Chemistry lessons and still lose a large number of marks when numbers appear. **HKDSE Chemistry calculations** combine chemical meaning, equations, units, ratios and algebra, so one weak stage affects the entire solution.

The answer is not simply “practise more calculations”. First identify which stage is failing.

## Find the exact calculation weakness

Check whether your child struggles to:

- write or use a balanced equation
- convert mass, amount and molar mass
- follow mole ratios
- work with concentration or gas quantities
- handle units and powers of ten
- identify the limiting reactant
- calculate percentage yield, purity or uncertainty
- decide which information is actually needed

A page of mixed questions can hide these differences. Group errors by skill.

## Use chemical meaning before formulas

Students often collect equations without understanding the quantities they connect. Before calculating, ask:

**What substance am I finding? What information do I have? How are the substances related in the reaction?**

Writing the balanced equation and marking the relevant quantities makes the route clearer.

## Follow a consistent structure

For each problem:

1. Write the equation or governing relationship.
2. Convert the given value to the required quantity.
3. Apply the correct chemical ratio.
4. Convert to the quantity requested.
5. Include units and sensible precision.

Clear working helps the student find the point of failure instead of restarting the whole question.

## Separate maths fluency from Chemistry

Short drills on rearranging, standard form, percentages and unit conversions can remove unnecessary difficulty. Then those skills must be placed back into chemical questions.

The official [HKEAA Chemistry subject page](https://www.hkeaa.edu.hk/en/hkdse/assessment/subject_information/category_a_subjects/chem/) should guide the level and assessment style of practice.

## Correct by reattempting

Reading a worked solution is not enough. The student should close it, reproduce the method and later answer a new question using the same reasoning.
