Parents looking for an **online Year 6 SATs tutor** often ask for “Maths and English help.” That is a useful start, but it is too broad to guide the first lesson. Your child may need help with fractions and multi-step word problems, or they may read well but find inference questions difficult.

## Find the exact bottleneck

Look at a recent school assessment and circle patterns rather than just counting marks. In Maths, separate calculation mistakes from questions where the child could not choose a method. In English, separate word reading, comprehension, spelling, grammar and writing. Ask the school what it has already identified.

One example: if a child can calculate 3 × 8 but cannot solve “three boxes hold eight pencils each,” more times-table drills alone may miss the point. Teach them to represent the situation with a diagram before calculating.

## What should a tutor do in the first two weeks?

The first lesson should include a small diagnostic, direct teaching of one priority skill and a brief check that the child can use it independently. In week two, revisit that skill before adding another. The [Education Endowment Foundation's overview of one-to-one tuition](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/one-to-one-tuition) emphasises matching teaching to pupils' understanding and giving feedback.

A routine of two short practice sessions between tutoring lessons may be easier to sustain than an exhausting pack of papers. Keep the child's confidence in view: SATs preparation is only one part of their learning.

## Questions for a potential online tutor

- Will you use my child's school curriculum and recent work?
- Can you explain how you would teach a missed word problem or inference question?
- How will I see improvement beyond a score?
- What should we practise at home without repeating the entire lesson?

If your child needs support in just one area, say so. A targeted plan is usually clearer than an indefinite “cover everything” package. Bring their recent work to a [free consultation](#consultation) to discuss a suitable Maths or English starting point.
