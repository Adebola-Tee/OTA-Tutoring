Registration for the **2027 Hong Kong Diploma of Secondary Education Examination (HKDSE)** is now open.

Students intending to sit the examination next year can register between **11 September and 7 October 2026**, with examination fees due by **11:59 p.m. on 14 October 2026**.

Whether your child is entering through a school or registering as a private candidate, these are the dates families should not miss.

## 2027 HKDSE registration deadline

The main registration period is:

**11 September – 7 October 2026**

The deadline for paying examination fees is:

**14 October 2026 at 11:59 p.m.**

Candidates who miss the normal payment deadline may make late payment between **15 and 20 October 2026**, but HKEAA states that an additional **HK$321 supplementary fee** will apply.

So registration and payment should ideally be completed early.

## Who can register for the 2027 HKDSE?

There are two main routes.

### School candidates

For the 2027 examination, school candidates must generally be bona fide **Secondary 6 students in the 2026/27 school year** studying at a registered school approved to participate in the HKDSE.

Schools normally handle the registration process with their students.

### Private candidates

A person can normally register directly as a private candidate when they:

* have previously sat an HKDSE; or
* will be **19 years old by 1 January 2027**.

Students who do not meet either condition may still qualify through the **special-entry route**, particularly where they have taken an equivalent public examination or are completing a non-HKDSE curriculum equivalent to Secondary 6.

Those applications are considered individually by HKEAA.

## What if your child studies an international curriculum?

This is particularly important for families whose children are studying outside the standard HKDSE school route.

A student following another Secondary 6-equivalent curriculum may need to apply for **special entry as a private candidate** and provide documents showing their academic background and curriculum.

The special-entry application period for the 2027 HKDSE runs until **7 October 2026**.

HKEAA states that special-entry applicants must also complete the normal HKDSE registration process separately. The information is not automatically transferred between the two systems.

That is an easy step to overlook.

## What are the 2027 HKDSE examination fees?

The total amount depends on factors including:

* whether the student is a school or private candidate
* the subjects being taken
* the candidate's status
* whether supplementary services or late applications are needed

HKEAA has already published the **2027 HKDSE examination and supplementary fees** and provides an online examination-fee calculator for candidates who want to estimate their total cost.

One additional cost worth noting is for special entry: the **2027 Special Entry Application Fee is HK$2,500**.

This is separate from the normal examination fees.

## How do private candidates register?

Private candidates register through the **HKDSE Examination Online Services**.

Before submitting the application, prepare the required identification and supporting information carefully.

For candidates applying through special entry, documents may include:

* valid identification
* evidence of academic qualifications
* school transcripts or certificates
* evidence of the curriculum being studied
* parental or guardian consent where applicable

HKEAA can also request additional supporting information depending on the applicant's circumstances.

Do not leave document preparation until 7 October.

## Important 2027 HKDSE dates

Parents and students should save these dates:

* **Registration:** 11 September – 7 October 2026
* **Exam fee deadline:** 14 October 2026 at 11:59 p.m.
* **Late payment period:** 15–20 October 2026
* **Late entry/subject amendment period:** 16 November – 4 December 2026, tentatively
* **Category A written examinations:** 6 April – 3 May 2027, tentatively
* **2027 HKDSE results:** 14 July 2027, tentatively

The examination may still feel months away, but registration is happening **now**.

## Registration is only the beginning

Once registration is completed, the next question should be:

**Is the student academically on track for the grades they need?**

September and October are valuable months for identifying weak topics before serious examination-paper practice begins.

Students struggling with Mathematics, English or other academic subjects should not wait until early 2027 to discover that major gaps remain.
