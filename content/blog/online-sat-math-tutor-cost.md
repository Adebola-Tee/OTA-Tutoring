An online SAT Math tutor may quote by the session, by the hour or for a package. Before deciding which offer is good value, establish what happens between the first practice result and the next independent attempt. The useful service is the teaching and feedback in that gap, not just access to more questions.

## Ask what the starting assessment involves

A broad Math score can hide very different difficulties. One learner may need equation fluency; another may know the content but misread the requested quantity. Ask how the tutor uses original working and official practice evidence to distinguish those cases.

A diagnostic does not always need to be another full test. A few well-chosen tasks and a discussion of recent mistakes may reveal a practical first priority. Confirm whether any formal assessment is included in the quoted arrangement or offered separately.

College Board's [SAT Math overview](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/sat/whats-on-the-test/math) is the official reference for the assessed mathematical areas. Preparation should reflect that test rather than be sold as a generic course covering every school topic.

## Compare teaching time fairly

Write down each session's duration and whether the student receives individual attention. A short appointment and a longer one can have different purposes. An hourly equivalent helps comparison, but the actual arrangement still needs to suit the learner.

For example, a lesson on a linear model might begin by distinguishing a fixed charge from a rate. The student then creates an equation, interprets its graph and checks a changed situation independently. Ask how the quoted time supports that sequence.

A recording can help revision, but it is not the same service as a live teacher responding to a student's incomplete attempt. Compare like with like before judging the fee.

## Find out how practice is reviewed

Ask what the learner receives between lessons and whether the tutor reviews the working. A completed question set offers little benefit if the same misconceptions remain unexplained.

Review should distinguish a wrong setup, a calculation error and a correct answer obtained by guessing. Those observations can change the next session. Ask whether feedback and any parent update are included, rather than assume that homework automatically means individual marking.

Our [SAT preparation programme guide](online-sat-math-preparation-programme.html) explains what a connected lesson plan could look like. Use it to compare the service rather than buy solely on the number of practice papers supplied.

## Check the full package commitment

Confirm the number of sessions, period covered and payment arrangement. Ask about rescheduling, missed dates and whether unused sessions expire. An intensive plan near a test may cost more in total even when the session rate stays unchanged.

Discuss the student's actual test date and realistic practice time. A tutor should explain what can be prioritised without claiming that a particular number of hours guarantees a particular score.

Avoid assuming a package contains a discount, unlimited support or test-registration assistance. Request its specific inclusions and decide whether those match what your family wants.
