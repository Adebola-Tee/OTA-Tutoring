The **UAE school year 2026–27 is officially underway**, and parents are already planning around school holidays, exams and family travel.

For schools following the UAE Ministry of Education curriculum, students returned on **31 August 2026**, making September the first full month of the new academic year. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

Here are the dates parents need to know.

## UAE School Calendar 2026–2027: Key Dates

**School year begins**  
31 August 2026

**First mid-term break**  
12–18 October 2026  
Students return on 19 October.

**Winter break**  
Begins 14 December 2026  
Students return on 4 January 2027.

**Spring break**  
5–11 April 2027  
Students return on 12 April.

**Last day of the academic year**  
2 July 2027. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

## What About Dubai Private Schools?

Parents in Dubai should remember that private-school calendars can vary slightly.

The Ministry allows some private schools to adjust their mid-term breaks, subject to approval from the relevant local education authority. Individual Dubai schools therefore publish their own approved calendars through KHDA. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

For example, KHDA calendars for several Dubai schools show the **2026–27 academic year beginning on 31 August 2026**, with public holidays and school-specific breaks added separately. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

Always check your child's own school calendar before booking travel.

## Important UAE School Holidays in 2026

Alongside school breaks, parents should watch official UAE public-holiday announcements.

Dubai school calendars currently include **UAE National Day on 2–3 December 2026**, while other religious holidays remain subject to official government confirmation. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

This matters because public holidays can create shorter school weeks around already busy assessment periods.

## When Are UAE School Exams in 2026–27?

Exam dates depend on the curriculum and school.

For Ministry-curriculum schools, the official calendar includes formal assessment periods throughout the year. The Ministry's published Term 3 schedule, for example, includes **mock examinations from 14–18 June 2027** and **end-of-term examinations from 21–30 June 2027**. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

International schools following **British, Cambridge, Edexcel, IB, American or other curricula** may use different internal assessment and external-exam dates.

Parents should therefore check both:

- the school's academic calendar
- the relevant international exam-board timetable

## What Parents Should Plan for Now

September is the best time to identify problems before they become exam-season problems.

Check:

- which subjects your child finds difficult
- upcoming school assessments
- IGCSE, GCSE, A Level or IB examination plans
- holiday periods that can be used for focused revision
