The **2026–27 school year has started in the UAE**, and for international-school students, September is more important than it looks.

Students following the **British curriculum, IGCSE, IB or American curriculum** are not simply returning to school. Many are entering new subjects, harder coursework and examination years where weaknesses can become expensive to fix later.

For most UAE schools following the September academic cycle, students returned on **31 August 2026**. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Here is what parents should focus on now.

## Start by Knowing Which Academic Year Actually Matters

Not every school year carries the same pressure.

**British curriculum and IGCSE students**  
Years 10 and 11 are especially important because students are working toward external examinations.

**IB students**  
Diploma Programme students need to manage subject content, internal assessments, extended work and examination preparation at the same time.

**American curriculum students**  
Grades, coursework, GPA and future college requirements can all make consistent performance important from the beginning of the year.

The mistake is waiting until the first poor report before taking academic preparation seriously.

## Check Last Year's Weak Subjects Now

September is the best time to identify gaps.

Ask:

- Which subjects caused the most difficulty last year?
- Which topics does your child still avoid?
- Were Mathematics, English or Sciences consistently weaker?
- Is the student entering IGCSE, IB or another examination year?
- Did summer break cause previously learned material to fade?

A student who begins September with unresolved algebra or essay-writing problems may struggle much more once new content is added.

## IGCSE Students Should Start Exam Preparation Early

For students beginning or continuing **IGCSE in the UAE**, exam preparation should not start a few weeks before mocks.

Students need to understand:

- their exact Cambridge or Pearson Edexcel syllabus
- how questions are structured
- which topics carry the most difficulty
- how marks are awarded
- how to use past papers properly

The strongest IGCSE students build exam technique gradually while learning the syllabus.

## IB Students Need Organisation From Week One

IB becomes difficult quickly when students allow deadlines to pile up.

A simple September system should include:

- weekly subject review
- organised notes
- scheduled assignment time
- early clarification of difficult concepts
- consistent revision rather than last-minute study

The goal is to prevent small academic problems from turning into several simultaneous problems later in the term.

## British and American Curriculum Students Need Different Strategies

Do not use the same study plan for every curriculum.

British-curriculum students often need strong preparation for externally assessed exams and exam-board question styles.

American-curriculum students may need more continuous attention to assignments, tests, class grades and GPA performance.

Parents should understand **how their child's school actually measures achievement** before deciding what academic support is needed.

## Watch the First Six Weeks Carefully

The first few weeks of school usually reveal a lot.

Look for:

**Repeated homework difficulty**  
One difficult assignment is normal. Constant difficulty is not.

**Falling test scores**  
Early low scores can reveal foundational gaps.

**Taking unusually long to complete work**  
This often means the student understands less than they appear to.

**Avoiding a particular subject**  
Students frequently avoid subjects where they feel least confident.

**Teacher concerns**  
Do not wait several months if a teacher already identifies a weakness.

Early intervention is usually easier than trying to repair an entire term later.

## Build a Simple Weekly Academic Routine

Students do not need an exhausting timetable.

A strong school-week routine can include:

- reviewing lessons shortly after they are taught
- completing homework before it becomes urgent
- practising Mathematics or Sciences several times each week
- reading and writing regularly for language-heavy subjects
- using weekends to revisit difficult topics

Consistency matters more than occasional eight-hour revision sessions.

## When Should a Parent Consider Extra Tutoring?

Tutoring is most useful when it solves a specific problem.

Consider additional support when your child:

- repeatedly struggles with the same subject
- is entering an important examination year
- has noticeable gaps from the previous year
- needs stronger exam technique
- performs well in class but poorly in tests
- needs more individual explanation than school lessons provide

The aim should not be to overload the student with more work.

It should be to make their existing schoolwork **easier to understand and easier to perform well in**.

## Start Strong Before the Pressure Builds

The beginning of the **UAE school year 2026–27** is the easiest time to make changes.

By October, new topics, assessments and deadlines will already be accumulating.

Parents do not need to panic about September performance. They simply need to pay attention early.

A student who begins the year with clear routines, strong foundations and support where needed is far less likely to spend the rest of the year catching up.
