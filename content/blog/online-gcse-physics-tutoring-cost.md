A GCSE Physics tutoring quote should describe the support being offered. Help with equations, scientific explanations and practical reasoning can require different teaching, even when two students have the same predicted grade.

At OTA Learning Studio, fees are confirmed privately after discussing the learner's course and goals. A [free parent consultation](#consultation) lets you ask about paid online Physics lessons, available Google Meet times and class packages. There is no fixed public Physics rate or guaranteed grade outcome on this page.

## Confirm the qualification before comparing fees

Ask whether the student takes Physics as a separate GCSE or studies Physics within Combined Science. Confirm the exam board, tier and year too. A provider needs those details to choose appropriate content and assessment materials.

The [AQA GCSE Physics overview](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/physics/gcse/physics-8463/specification/specification-at-a-glance) describes that separate qualification. It does not define every GCSE or Combined Science course. Ask the school for the actual entry rather than infer it from the word triple in a conversation.

The quote should relate to the service your child needs. A general homework session is not automatically the same as a planned sequence of exam preparation with independent checks.

## Ask what the teaching will address

Suppose a student calculates current from voltage and resistance correctly but cannot explain why changing the resistance changes the current. The next lesson may need to connect the relationship with the physical situation rather than repeat numerical substitutions.

Another learner might understand the explanation but mishandle units or rearrangement. A useful tutor investigates those distinctions before setting a package length. The initial priority should be clearer than improve all Physics.

Our guide to [choosing a GCSE Physics tutor](choose-online-gcse-physics-tutor.html) gives questions about the teaching. Use those alongside the price discussion so the fee does not become the only comparison point.

## Establish what is included outside contact time

Ask whether the arrangement includes selected practice, feedback and parent updates. A worksheet has a purpose when its attempts influence the next lesson. Merely supplying more downloadable resources does not show whether a misconception is being repaired.

For practical questions, feedback may examine variables, controls, measurements or evaluation of a method. For explanations, it may address a missing causal link. Find out how the teacher reviews those responses and whether that review is included.

Also confirm how the student's written work is shown during the appointment. A live individual lesson should allow the tutor to respond to the learner's thinking, not only present a prepared slide sequence.

## Compare the total arrangement

Record the session duration, fee, number of appointments and period covered. One weekly lesson and an intensive short block are different commitments, even if their hourly equivalent is similar.

Clarify payment, missed dates, rescheduling and any package conditions before booking. Do not assume a package includes a discount or unlimited contact. Ask for the actual service and terms available.

Consider the learner's other subjects too. More sessions have value when each has a purpose and leaves room for independent practice. A packed timetable without time to use feedback may be difficult to sustain.

## Decide what would make the lessons useful

An early goal might be choosing an equation from a situation, explaining an energy transfer or evaluating a practical method using evidence. Ask how the tutor will check that goal with unfamiliar work after teaching.
