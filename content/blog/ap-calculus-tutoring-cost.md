## Identify AB or BC in the enquiry

Tell the tutor the exact course and assessment year. BC includes additional material, so someone offering general calculus support should explain whether their teaching fits the student's current topics.

College Board's [AP Calculus AB course overview](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-calculus-ab) also flags exam updates for May 2027. Check preparation against the intended examination rather than assume every older practice paper has the current timing and question count.

A tutor's usefulness depends on course fit and clear explanations, not simply how many advanced topics appear in a profile. Ask about the specific area your child finds difficult.

## Ask what an individual lesson would address

Suppose the student knows that the derivative of x² is 2x but cannot explain a negative derivative in context. A lesson might connect a graph, rate of change and a written interpretation before moving to another calculation technique.

A BC learner may need to distinguish whether a series converges and what its sum would be if it does. Those are different questions. The tutor should investigate which decision is missing rather than assign increasingly long calculations.

Our guide to [choosing an AP Calculus BC tutor](choose-online-ap-calculus-bc-tutor.html) illustrates how a course-specific difficulty can shape the teaching. That explanation helps you judge what a quote is actually buying.

## Compare contact time and feedback

Establish the duration of each appointment, whether it is individual and how the student participates. A live session responding to unfinished working is different from access to recorded lessons or a group revision event.

Ask whether selected practice and review are included. A learner might reproduce a method during a lesson yet need help when it appears in a graph or unfamiliar context later. Feedback should reveal that distinction and influence the next session.

Do not assume every package includes unlimited marking or contact outside lessons. Request the actual arrangements so that two quotes can be compared fairly.

## Understand the total commitment

Write down the lesson fee, session count and period covered. Ask whether a package is paid in advance, how missed dates are handled and whether there are any expiry or rescheduling conditions.

If more frequent teaching is proposed, ask what each session would do and when the learner will practise independently. More contact can have a purpose, but it should not automatically replace every opportunity to work without hints.

A student balancing several AP courses also needs a manageable week. An expensive intensive plan may be unsuitable even when the family can afford it, while a focused regular arrangement may fit a narrower gap.

## Decide on a first goal before a long plan

A useful starting goal could be interpreting a definite integral, choosing a convergence test or justifying a statement from derivative information. Ask how the goal will be checked with new questions after teaching.
