Registration for the **Math Kangaroo 2027 Individual Competition** is now open for students across the United States.

The competition is open to **Grades K–12** and will take place on **Thursday, 18 March 2027**. This is also a special year for Math Kangaroo USA, marking its **30th Individual Competition**. ([mathkangaroo.org](https://mathkangaroo.org/mks/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

For parents considering registering their child, the most important thing is not simply securing a place.

It is understanding **how registration works, which level your child must enter and how to use the months before March to prepare effectively**.

## Math Kangaroo 2027 registration dates

The main student registration period runs from:

**15 September – 31 December 2026**

Late registration is available from:

**1 January – 1 February 2027**

The competition takes place on:

**18 March 2027**. ([mathkangaroo.org](https://mathkangaroo.org/mks/resources/how-to-start-math-kangaroo-center/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

Parents should register early because some centres can reach capacity before the final deadline. Math Kangaroo itself notes that demand can exceed the number of available places in some areas. ([mathkangaroo.org](https://mathkangaroo.org/mks/registration/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

## Who can take Math Kangaroo?

Math Kangaroo is open to **students from Kindergarten through Grade 12**.

A child must compete at their **actual school grade level**. Students are not permitted to enter at a lower level simply because the questions may feel easier. ([mathkangaroo.org](https://mathkangaroo.org/mks/registration/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

This makes preparation especially important.

Math Kangaroo is not simply a test of whether a student can perform routine school calculations.

The competition is built around **mathematical thinking, logic and problem-solving**.

## How much does Math Kangaroo 2027 cost?

Math Kangaroo has published a lower fee during the regular registration period and a higher fee for late registration.

For the 2027 competition, its official information currently lists the late registration fee as **$35 per student** through 1 February 2027. Families should confirm the regular-registration amount shown during checkout because Math Kangaroo’s current official pages display differing regular-fee information. ([mathkangaroo.org](https://mathkangaroo.org/mks/registration/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)) ([mathkangaroo.org](https://mathkangaroo.org/mks/resources/how-to-start-math-kangaroo-center/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

Registering during the regular period is therefore the safest option both for availability and cost.

## Where can students take the competition?

Families can choose from several participation formats depending on availability.

These can include:

* in-person centres
* school-based centres
* proctored online centres
* remote participation
* virtual self-proctored participation

Public centres accept students from different schools, while private centres normally require an invitation code from the school or organisation hosting the competition. ([mathkangaroo.org](https://mathkangaroo.org/mks/registration/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

Math Kangaroo also provides a searchable list of **2027 competition centres across the United States**. ([mathkangaroo.org](https://mathkangaroo.org/mks/2027-individual-competition-centers/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

Parents should check centre availability early instead of assuming the nearest location will remain open until December.

## How should students prepare for Math Kangaroo?

This is where parents should be careful.

Math Kangaroo preparation should not become endless arithmetic worksheets.

The competition rewards children who can:

* recognise patterns
* reason logically
* interpret diagrams
* solve unfamiliar problems
* choose efficient strategies
* stay calm when the answer is not immediately obvious

A child may be strong in school Maths and still find Math Kangaroo questions challenging because the **style of thinking is different**.

Start with age-appropriate competition-style problems and watch how your child approaches them.

Do they understand the question but choose the wrong method?

Do they give up when a problem looks unfamiliar?

Do they rush?

Do they struggle with multi-step reasoning?

Those observations are more useful than simply counting correct answers.

## Do not wait until March to begin preparation

Students registering in September have roughly six months before the competition.

That is plenty of time when preparation is structured.

A simple progression could be:

**September–November:** strengthen problem-solving foundations and weaker Maths topics.

**December–January:** introduce more Math Kangaroo-style questions and reasoning practice.

**February–March:** increase timed practice, review mistakes and improve competition technique.

The aim is not to turn every week into exam preparation.

It is to gradually make challenging Maths feel more familiar.

## Is Math Kangaroo suitable for your child?

Math Kangaroo can be valuable for children who enjoy Maths, but it can also help pupils who need to develop stronger **reasoning and problem-solving confidence**.

The most useful question is not whether your child is already a “Maths genius”.

It is whether they can be taught to **think through unfamiliar problems instead of becoming stuck when the method is not obvious**.

That skill is useful far beyond one competition.
