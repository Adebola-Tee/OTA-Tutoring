Privacy information

Clear information about your data.

OTA Learning Studio collects only the information needed to respond to enquiries, arrange consultations and provide tutoring support.

Last updated: 30 July 2026

This notice explains the current data practices of the OTA Learning Studio website and the external services used for enquiries, bookings and communication.

Information you choose to provide

You may provide your name, email address, learner level, subject, curriculum, availability and learning goals when booking a consultation or contacting OTA Learning Studio. Please avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information.

How the information is used

  • To respond to enquiries and arrange consultations.
  • To understand the learner’s academic needs.
  • To plan and deliver agreed tutoring services.
  • To send relevant scheduling or lesson communication.
  • To maintain appropriate administrative and safeguarding records where necessary.

Booking through Cal.com

Consultations are scheduled using Cal.com. When you open the booking link or submit booking information, Cal.com processes that information under its own terms and privacy practices. OTA Learning Studio receives the appointment details needed to conduct the consultation.

Email communication

If you email taiwo@otalearningstudio.com, the message and reply details are processed through the relevant email provider. They are used for tutoring-related communication and administration.

Live chat through Tawk.to

The website uses Tawk.to to provide live chat and offline messaging. If you use the chat, Tawk.to may process the information you enter, together with technical details needed to operate and secure the service. OTA Learning Studio receives the conversation so that it can respond to your enquiry. Please do not send sensitive personal, medical or financial information through live chat.

WhatsApp communication

The WhatsApp button opens a conversation with OTA Learning Studio through WhatsApp. Messages, profile details and related technical information are processed under WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy practices. Using WhatsApp is optional; email and secure online booking remain available.

Children’s information

Parents or guardians should make initial enquiries and booking arrangements for children. Learner information should be limited to what is reasonably needed to discuss and deliver educational support.

Cookies and analytics

The website does not currently use its own marketing analytics or advertising pixels. Tawk.to, WhatsApp, Cal.com and other external services may use cookies or similar technologies under their own privacy practices. This notice will be updated before OTA Learning Studio activates additional website analytics or advertising tracking.

Sharing and retention

Information is not sold. It may be processed by service providers used for email, scheduling, video lessons or file delivery, only as needed to provide the service. Information should be kept only for as long as reasonably needed for communication, service delivery, safeguarding, accounting or legal obligations.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information held directly by OTA Learning Studio, subject to any legitimate record-keeping obligation. Email taiwo@otalearningstudio.com with the subject “Privacy Request”.

Changes to this notice

This page will be updated when website functions or data practices change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
