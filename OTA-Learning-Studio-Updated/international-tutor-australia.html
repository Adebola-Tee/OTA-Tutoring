Common questions

Before lessons begin.

Do you teach the Australian national curriculum?

OTA Learning Studio’s specialist focus is international curricula and exams: IB, IGCSE, British pathways and SAT preparation. Please share the exact programme during the consultation.

Can a student combine subject tutoring with SAT support?

Yes. A plan can strengthen school Maths or English foundations while reserving focused sessions for test-specific strategy and timed work.

Will the booking times appear correctly in Australia?

Yes. The booking calendar automatically displays availability in the visitor’s local timezone.
